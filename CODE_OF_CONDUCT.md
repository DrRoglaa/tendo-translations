# Code of Conduct

I want this project to be a friendly place where people can ask questions without feeling foolish. Translation is full of nuance, and even native speakers can disagree about the best phrasing.

Please be respectful, assume good intent, welcome people of different backgrounds and experience levels, and criticize wording rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, repeated personal attacks, and sharing someone else's private information aren't welcome.

When reviewing suggestions, explain *why* something reads better in context. A little humor is welcome; sarcasm aimed at contributors isn't.

If there is a problem, contact me privately using the maintainer contact details on Tendo's official support page, or open a public Issue only if it is safe to do so. Do not post sensitive reports or anyone's personal details in Issues. I may remove inappropriate contributions or restrict participation when needed.

Thank you for helping me keep this place thoughtful and useful.
