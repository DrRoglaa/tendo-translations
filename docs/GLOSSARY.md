## Context notes

- **Slovenian:** check dual (`two`) as well as singular, few and other. Counts ending in 01/02/03 require careful handling.
- **Croatian:** use appropriate Croatian forms and singular/few/other categories.
- **German:** allow for long compound words, natural cases and appropriate voice.
- **Italian:** maintain natural phrasing and consistent gender/article agreement.
- **Hungarian:** word order and suffixes often differ from English.
- **Japanese and Korean:** keep labels compact, natural, and appropriate to product UI; don't import English grammar.

The brand name **Tendo** should remain unchanged. Siri's `${applicationName}` token and all format arguments must remain intact. The official names of Apple features should follow local platform terminology. Please suggest glossary corrections through an Issue with examples of real on-screen use.
