The "translated" state in Xcode means a technical draft exists; it is **not** a human sign-off.
Even a reviewed catalog does not establish Tendo's public app language availability.

**How to help:** Pick a language and topic, then [report an issue](https://github.com/DrRoglaa/tendo-translations/issues/new/choose) or [contribute a single-language correction](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).
Review batches are internal key groups for tracking approvals; they do not require separate Markdown files.

**Source of truth:** [`locales/`](../../locales/) and the protected [review evidence](../../review/README.md). These generated pages may lag recently merged corrections until refreshed by a maintainer.
